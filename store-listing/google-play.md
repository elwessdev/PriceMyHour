## Full description

Stop guessing what to charge. Freelance Rate Calculator works out the minimum hourly rate you need to hit your income goal after platform fees, taxes and business expenses, then turns it into a project price you can quote with confidence.

Enter what you want to take home each month, how much you really work and what it costs you to run your business. The app does the math instantly and shows exactly where every dollar goes, so you never underprice a job again.

KEY FEATURES

• Minimum hourly rate, daily rate and monthly gross, updated live as you type
• Accounts for vacation days, holidays and non-billable time (admin, proposals, learning)
• Platform fee quick picks: Upwork 10%, Fiverr 20% or Direct 0%, or enter your own
• Includes tax rate, monthly business expenses and a profit/safety margin
• Visual breakdown of how much goes to taxes, expenses, fees and your pocket
• Project Estimator: enter hours plus a buffer and get a clean fixed price
• See what the client pays versus what you actually receive after fees
• Copy a ready-to-send quote to your clipboard in one tap
• Save presets for different platforms, clients or niches and load them anytime
• Multiple currencies: USD, EUR, GBP, TND or your own custom symbol
• Light and dark mode that follows your system setting

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• No account, no sign-up
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• No permissions requested
• Works 100% offline: all your data stays on your device

Whether you are a designer, developer, writer, consultant or virtual assistant, Freelance Rate Calculator helps you set rates that cover your costs and pay you what you are worth.

Estimates only. Not financial or tax advice.
