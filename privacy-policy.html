Privacy Policy

RateWise: Freelance Rate Calculator · Last updated: November 26, 2026

  • The app collects no personal data.
  • All data is stored locally on your device.

Information we collect

RateWise does not collect, transmit or share any personal information. The app has no user accounts, does not require sign-in and requests no Android permissions.

0

Data stored on your device

The numbers you enter (such as your income target, working hours, fees, tax rate and expenses), your selected currency and any presets you save are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. You can delete it at any time by deleting presets in the app, clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstalling the app.

Third-party services

The app does not use any third-party services, SDKs for analytics or advertising, crash reporting or cloud storage. It works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Clipboard

When you tap "Copy quote", a short text summary is copied to your device's clipboard. The app only writes to the clipboard when you ask it to and never reads from it.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal data, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
